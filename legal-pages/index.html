Klipo

A video scheduling tool operated by Kerem Demir

Klipo helps you plan, schedule, and publish short videos to your own TikTok account. You prepare a video and its caption, choose who can view it and which interactions are allowed, pick a date and time, and Klipo publishes it for you through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

We do not collect data from visitors of this website. Data is handled only for people who sign in to the application and connect their own TikTok account, as described in the Privacy Policy.

What Klipo does

  • Connects to your TikTok account through TikTok's official sign-in and consent screen.
  • Lets you upload videos, write captions, and schedule them on a calendar.
  • Lets you edit or delete a scheduled post before it is published.
  • Shows basic account details and statistics for your own videos in the dashboard.

How the TikTok connection works

You connect your account through TikTok Login Kit. Klipo receives only the permissions shown on the TikTok consent screen and uses them to publish the content you have created and scheduled, and to show basic account details and video statistics inside the dashboard. Nothing is posted without your action, and you can remove the connection at any time.

Klipo is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.
