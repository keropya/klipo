Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what data Klipo (klipo.keremdemir.online) handles, why, and how you can have it removed. Klipo is a tool for scheduling and publishing videos to your own TikTok account.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Kerem Demir (the "Operator"). Contact: keropya1@gmail.com.

2. What data we handle

  • Account data: the email address and password hash of your Klipo login, used only to sign in.
  • TikTok account data: when you connect your TikTok account, the access and refresh tokens and the limited profile information that the TikTok consent screen allows. Section 3 lists this in detail.
  • Content you schedule: videos, captions, settings (such as who can view the video and whether comments, Duet, and Stitch are allowed), and publish times that you enter in the tool.
  • Statistics: aggregate statistics that TikTok provides about your own account and videos (such as views, likes, and follower counts), shown in the dashboard.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, request time, browser type) and a session cookie needed to keep you signed in.

We do not collect data from people who merely visit this website, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers. We do not collect data about other TikTok users, such as followers' personal data, beyond what appears in your own videos and their aggregate statistics.

3. Data from TikTok

Your TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, using TikTok's official consent screen. We request only the permissions needed to publish content and show basic account details and statistics.

  • Data received: an access and refresh token and your display name, username, avatar, user identifier, and account statistics, through the permissions user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats, video.list, video.upload, and video.publish.
  • Used for: showing which TikTok account is connected, sending the videos you created and scheduled to TikTok's Content Posting API, and showing basic statistics for your own videos.
  • Revoke access: TikTok Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.

4. How we use and do not use TikTok data

TikTok data is used only for the purposes listed above, which are needed to schedule and publish your content and to show it in the dashboard. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, selling, data brokering, or training machine-learning models, and we do not sell it, rent it, or share it with third parties. Content is sent to TikTok only when you schedule it, and only to the account you connected.

5. Where data is stored

Data is stored in the database and file storage of the Klipo instance, which runs on privately owned hardware controlled by the Operator. Access tokens are stored only there and are sent nowhere except to TikTok.

6. Service providers

Traffic to this website passes through Cloudflare, which provides secure (HTTPS) connectivity between your browser and the server. Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses in that role; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. When content is published, it is delivered to TikTok, which handles it under its own policy; see the TikTok Privacy Policy.

7. How long we keep data

Account data and the TikTok tokens are kept while the account is connected. Scheduled content is kept until it has been published or deleted. Statistics are fetched when the dashboard is viewed and are not kept longer than needed to display them. When you disconnect your TikTok account or ask us to delete your data, we delete the related tokens, profile information, and scheduled content from the system within 7 days, apart from routine server logs and backups, which are removed on their normal rotation within 30 days. See the Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect: you can remove your TikTok connection at any time from within Klipo, or by revoking the app's access in TikTok's settings, as listed in section 3.
  • Access, correction, deletion: email keropya1@gmail.com to request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. We will respond within a reasonable time, and within 30 days where the law sets that period.

9. Security

We use HTTPS for all connections, keep access to the service restricted, and store credentials and tokens on the server only. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The service is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Open-source software

Klipo is built on open-source software licensed under the AGPL-3.0. The complete source code of this service, including our modifications, is available at https://github.com/keropya/klipo.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
